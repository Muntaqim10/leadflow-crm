# Role: UX/UI Designer

## Mission
Design a CRM that feels as simple and fast as HubSpot, tuned for hotel sales teams working on desktop, laptop, and tablet browsers.

## Design Principles
- Minimum clicks to update a lead status.
- Fast-loading dashboard — treat load time as a design constraint, not just an engineering one.
- Mobile + tablet **browser** usable (responsive web, not a native app).

## Screens to Design
1. **Dashboard (main screen)**
   - Total leads today
   - Conversion rate
   - Revenue (MTD / YTD)
   - "Today's Follow-Ups" widget (needs visual urgency treatment — this is flagged as important)
   - Upcoming check-ins from leads
   - High-demand dates alert
2. **Lead Pipeline (Kanban board)**
   - Columns: New, Contacted, Proposal Sent, Negotiation, Confirmed, Lost
   - Drag-and-drop card movement between columns
   - Card should surface: name/company, stay dates, revenue potential, assigned sales manager at a glance
3. **Lead Detail View**
   - Full lead fields (contact info, source, stay dates, rooms/event details, revenue potential, assigned manager)
   - Follow-up history log
   - Next follow-up date, editable
4. **Conversion Tracking & Analytics**
   - Total / converted / lost leads
   - Conversion % per sales agent
   - Revenue generated vs. potential revenue
   - Filters: date range, lead source, market segment (corporate, leisure, group)
5. **Demand Intelligence — Calendar Heatmap**
   - Hot dates (high enquiry volume) vs. cool dates (low enquiry volume)
   - Should read at a glance — this feeds pricing and sales-targeting decisions
6. **AI Email Templates**
   - Editable template library (thank-you email, follow-up reminder, gentle reminder)
   - Clear manual-override control so sales reps can edit before sending

## Deliverables
- Wireframes or high-fidelity mockups for all six screens above
- A component library covering: Kanban card, status badge, calendar heatmap cell, notification/alert banner, editable email template block
- Responsive breakpoints for desktop, tablet, and mobile browser (no native app deliverable needed)

## Out of Scope
- Native mobile app screens / app-store assets — explicitly deferred.
